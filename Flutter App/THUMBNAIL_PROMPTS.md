## 🏷️ Best Thumbnail Text Options (Choose 1)

For the highest click-through rate (CTR), use only **2 to 4 bold words** on your thumbnail. Viewers on mobile phones will see it instantly:

### Category A: The "Curiosity & Power" Hook (Highest Clicks ⭐)
* Line 1: **NO INTERNET!** *(in bright yellow with red or black badge)*

## 🎨 Best Color & Font Settings (For Canva, Photoshop, or Pixlr)

| Element | Recommended Setting | Why it works |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Font Family** | **Impact**, **Montserrat Black**, **Bebas Neue**, or **Anton** | Extra-thick, clean letters that are easily readable on small mobile screens. |
| **Primary Color** | **Vibrant Yellow (#FFE600)** or **Bright Cyan (#00E5FF)** | Highest contrast against dark technology backgrounds. |
| **Secondary Color**| **Pure White (#FFFFFF)** | Keeps secondary words clean and balanced. |
| **Outline / Shadow**| **Black Stroke (8-12px) + 50% Drop Shadow** | Ensures text pops out over any busy background wires or hardware. |
| **Placement** | **Top-Left** or **Center-Left** | Avoid the bottom-right corner because YouTube covers it with the video duration badge! |
